PlainPrint

100% Local Processing • Wi-Fi & USB OTG Printing

PlainPrint is a lightweight, privacy-first Android printing utility that lets you print PDFs, documents, and photos directly to local Wi-Fi (IPP / AirPrint / Port 9100) and USB OTG printers without cloud uploads.
