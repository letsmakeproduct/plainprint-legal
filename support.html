Need help connecting your Wi-Fi or USB OTG printer, or want to report a bug? Reach out anytime at letsmakeproduct.hq@gmail.com.

Quick Troubleshooting Tips

  • Wi-Fi Printer Not Found: Ensure your Android phone and printer are connected to the exact same Wi-Fi network (not a guest network with client isolation enabled).
  • USB OTG Printing: Use an OTG-capable USB adapter/cable and tap Allow when Android prompts for USB device permission.
