Privacy Policy for PlainPrint

Effective Date: November 14, 2026 • Contact: letsmakeproduct.hq@gmail.com

PlainPrint ("we", "our", or "the App") is built with a strict local-first architecture. Your documents, PDFs, and photos remain entirely on your Android device and are transmitted directly to your selected local printer.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

PlainPrint does not collect, store, transmit, or upload any of the following to external cloud servers:

  • Documents, PDFs, images, or print job contents
  • Personal identification details, names, phone numbers, or account logins
  • Precise GPS location history or contacts

2. Device Permissions & Why They Are Used

  • Storage / Photo & Document Picker: Used strictly to let you select the local PDF, image, or document file you choose to print.
  • Local Network / Wi-Fi Access: Used solely to discover printers on your local Wi-Fi network (via mDNS/Bonjour, IPP, or Raw TCP Port 9100) and send print data directly to your printer.
  • USB Host (USB OTG): Used solely to communicate directly with a printer connected to your Android device via a USB cable.

3. Data Retention & Local Storage

Temporary print render caches created during active print jobs are stored locally in the app's private cache directory on your device and are automatically cleared or removed when you clear app cache or uninstall PlainPrint.

4. Third-Party Services

The current version of PlainPrint does not integrate third-party analytics, user-tracking SDKs, or cloud document-conversion APIs. If future updates introduce optional services (such as advertising), this Privacy Policy and the Google Play Data Safety section will be updated prior to release.

5. Children's Privacy

PlainPrint is a general productivity utility not directed toward children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

6. Changes & Contact

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. If you have questions about privacy or data handling, contact us at letsmakeproduct.hq@gmail.com.
